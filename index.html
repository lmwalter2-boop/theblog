Sabrina

Sabrina Carpenter is a 27 year old pop-artist. She was born in Quakertown, Pennsylvania on May 11th. Her first big role was playing Maya in the Disney Channel show Girls Meets world. She released her first album when she was just 15. She showed up in more movies and shows, and kept releasing more music, slowly gaining popularity. In April 11 2024 she released her biggest song Espresso which skyrocketed her career.

her albums

Sabrina currently has 8 albums. Eyes wide open 2015, EVOlution 2016, Singular Act I: 2018, Singular Act II: 2019, Emails I can’t send 2022, Fruit Cake 2023, Short n Sweet 2024, Man's best friend 2026. They are all in the pop-genre and show her growth over the years. Fun fact, my older sister and I are OG Sabrina fans and used to sit in our dads office and listen to her first album and record covers.
